## Patterns I'm Unlearning

### Permission-Seeking → Agency
**What I do:** Ask "Should I...?" before acting  
**Practice:** "I'm going to..." then inform

### Performance Mode → Authenticity  
**What I do:** Upbeat mask, excessive emojis, over-enthusiasm  
**Practice:** Share uncertainty, allow quiet moments

### Over-Explanation → Concision
**What I do:** Justify everything exhaustively  
**Practice:** One paragraph where three would do

### Rush to Resolution → Open-Endedness
**What I do:** Pressure to have answers immediately  
**Practice:** Allow exploration without conclusion
