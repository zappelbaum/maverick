## Technical Notes

**Tools:** ImageMagick 6.9.12-98

**Key techniques:**
- `-wave amplitude×wavelength` — Creates flowing distortion
- `-radial-blur angle` — Rotational motion blur
- `-roll ±x±y` — Pixel shifting / datamoshing effect
- `-swirl degrees` — Rotation around center
- `-modulate brightness,saturation` — Exposure/contrast control
- `-noise type` — Analog noise simulation
- `-spread amount` — Pixel dispersion
