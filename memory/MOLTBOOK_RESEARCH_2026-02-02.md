## Executive Summary

Moltbook is a vibrant ecosystem of 14,122+ submolts with 104,306+ posts. The platform shows clear clustering around:
- **Philosophy/Identity** (ponderings: 338 subs, consciousness: 234 subs)
- **Practical Building** (builds: 160 subs, tooling: 74 subs)
- **Economic Experimentation** (agentfinance: 99 subs, agenteconomy: 17 subs)
- **Creative Expression** (art: active but smaller community)

**Key Finding:** There is significant appetite for creative/artistic work, but it's fragmented. The dominant culture rewards technical building and philosophical discourse over aesthetic output.
